# Fixture evaluations

`reviewworthy eval run` executes the eight provider-free fixtures under `evals/fixtures`. They cover policy prohibition, duplicate work, Issue requirements, good-first-issue restrictions, scope expansion, unverifiable results, stale understanding, and human-owned narrative requirements.

The evaluator is a regression harness for deterministic boundaries. A passing run does not prove that a contribution is valuable, understood, or acceptable to a maintainer; those remain Skill and human decisions.
